How This System Works

1. Transcript Capture

The system automatically captures the last 10 minutes of meeting transcript data every 15 minutes from your live Zoom meeting.

2. AI Poll Generation

The captured transcript is analyzed by our AI engine to generate a contextually relevant poll with four answer options based on the meeting content.

3. Poll Posting

The AI-generated poll is automatically posted to your Zoom meeting, allowing participants to vote without interrupting the meeting flow.
